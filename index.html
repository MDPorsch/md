Experience

Founder — Breachrr

2026 – Present Remote

Continuous credential exposure monitoring for small companies — the kind of security tooling that used to require a Fortune 500 budget. Shipped a working product to market as a solo founder covering backend, frontend, security, and infrastructure.

  • Designed a continuous scanning pipeline monitoring breach databases, infostealer malware feeds, public code repositories, and domain infrastructure — surfacing exposures within seconds of detection.

Information Security Lead — Verixar Ltd.

2026 – Present London, UK · Remote

Sole security lead at a seed-stage InsurTech building a customer data verification and fraud prevention platform. Responsible for designing and implementing the company’s entire information security program from scratch.

  • Delivered a working security program — Information Security Policy, Cyber Incident Response Plan, Business Continuity & Disaster Recovery Plan, and Cyber Security Risk Register — without pulling engineering time away from the product.
  • Ran the company’s first formal security assessment and penetration test, delivered a versioned findings report, and closed every tracked remediation before sign-off.
  • Leading the NCSC Cyber Essentials certification program, mapping and implementing controls across all five domains: firewalls, secure configuration, access control, malware protection, and patch management.
  • Established and enforced MFA across all company accounts and platforms; hardened a fully BYOD environment (full disk encryption, screen lock, Cloudflare DNS filtering) without an MDM.
  • Conducted secret scanning across all repositories, produced the first security awareness training program, and stood up a vendor risk assessment framework for every new integration.
